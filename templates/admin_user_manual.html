ODM 2027 Nomination System User Manual

TRAINING / SIMULATION ONLY

This protected online guide summarizes the operating sequence. Use the downloadable Word manual for complete procedures, checklists, environment variables and the Admin Housekeeping appendix.

1 Voter and membership registration

  1. Register the member in the authorized Kobo form with National ID, full name, ODM registration number, county, constituency, ward, polling station and clear photographs.
  2. The system checks live Kobo first, then membership_registration.csv in Kobo media. Mark a person unregistered only when absent from both.
  3. Maintain the CSV from Admin Data Files and test live-Kobo and CSV-only records after replacement.
  4. Generate the protected voters register, filter from county_main.csv, and distribute only the relevant polling-centre copy.

2 Candidate registration and approval

  1. The applicant verifies National ID and registered phone, then completes one private application.
  2. President is national; Governor, Senator and Women Representative are county-restricted; MNA is constituency-restricted; MCA is ward-restricted.
  3. Candidate photograph and payment evidence are mandatory.
  4. An administrator records Pending, Accepted or Rejected. Only Active and Accepted candidates enter ballots.
  5. Mark the reviewed list Final and lock it. Only a Level 2 administrator may unlock under documented authority.

3 Entrance verification

  1. The assigned officer enters the National ID and compares the person with the ID and passport photographs.
  2. Confirm the correct polling station and no prior vote.
  3. Select Identity Confirmed and Approve for Voting only after positive identification. Approval is short-lived and single-use.

4 Stream opening and voting

  1. Capture GPS, select the full hierarchy, confirm zero pre-cast votes, centrally lock the device to the stream, and print the signed opening report.
  2. For each voter, select Next Voter, enter ID, and select Check Approval and Verify Voter Photos.
  3. Do not proceed unless entrance approval, membership, station, photographs and prior-vote checks pass.
  4. The voter selects one candidate or Skip in each of six categories, reviews all decisions and submits once.

5 Closing, count and declaration

  1. Close only at the configured time and from the owning device.
  2. Review all six category totals, uniform registered voters, participation, skips and votes not cast; print and sign the closing reports.
  3. Confirm repository deposit and reconcile signed stream records.
  4. Use Winners and Runners-up for votes and percentage of valid candidate votes. Apply county filters for MNA and MCA when needed.
  5. Resolve exceptions, recounts and ties under approved party rules before signing and publishing a declaration.

6 Admin housekeeping summary

  • Check Render services, PostgreSQL, Kobo access, APIs, clocks and logs daily.
  • Back up every CSV before replacement; validate, upload, download the activated copy and test lookups.
  • Configure a persistent disk and DATA_UPLOAD_DIR for local managed uploads.
  • Disable reassigned officers, protect secrets, restrict Level 2 access and record every unlock.
  • Confirm every closed stream report, reconcile no-voting streams and preserve signed records and backups.
