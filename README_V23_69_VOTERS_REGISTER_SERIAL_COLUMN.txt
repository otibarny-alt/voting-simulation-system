V23.69 VOTERS REGISTER SERIAL NUMBER

The voters register now includes Serial No. in:
- the protected browser register;
- the downloadable CSV; and
- the printable landscape PDF.

Serial values are merged from live Kobo and membership_registration.csv.
Accepted CSV headings are serial_no, serial_number, and serial. The live Kobo
field defaults to basics/serial_no and can be changed with the Render variable
MEMBERSHIP_SERIAL_FIELD.
