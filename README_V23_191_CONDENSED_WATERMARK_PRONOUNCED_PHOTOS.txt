V23.191 - CONDENSED BALLOT WATERMARKS AND PRONOUNCED CANDIDATE PHOTOS

1. Emergency ballot watermarks now use 28 smaller ODM seals arranged in a
   compact 4-column by 7-row pattern across every A5 page.

   downloaded PDF.

   the photograph is the most visually prominent part of each candidate row.
4. Voting boxes remain large, clear and unobstructed.
