V23.205 — Unopposed candidates and direct nomination

1. A printable ballot now requires at least two approved candidates in the exact electoral scope.
2. A position with exactly one approved candidate is marked X and treated as unopposed/direct nomination.
3. Unopposed candidates remain in the electronic voting catalogue; only emergency paper-ballot preview and PDF printing are blocked.
4. Admin Data Files now links to an admin-protected Unopposed Candidates / Direct Nomination page.
5. The new page filters by county, constituency, ward and position and shows the candidate and exact electoral scope.
6. Voter-total web/PDF ballot checks and print quantities exclude unopposed positions.
