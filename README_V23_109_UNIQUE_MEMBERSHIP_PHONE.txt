V23.109 — UNIQUE MEMBERSHIP PHONE NUMBERS

New membership registrations and existing-member correction requests now
reject a phone number that belongs to another approved member or appears in
another pending membership request.

The phone is normalized before comparison, so Kenyan forms such as 0712345678,
712345678 and 254712345678 are treated as the same number. An existing member
may retain their own unchanged phone number.

The rule is enforced again when an administrator approves a request, preventing
a number assigned after submission from being duplicated. Candidate-based
membership locks and the existing editable-field restrictions remain unchanged.
